# SaySites Instagram pack (October 2026)

A fresh start for @saysites, matching SaySites today: premium websites for law firms, medical practices, med spas, dentists and home services, focused on leads. No prices anywhere.

## What's inside

- `profile/`: the profile photo (the SaySites mark).
- `posts/`: 9 feed posts (22 images; 5 are carousels), 1080×1350.
- `stories/`: 5 Stories, 1080×1920. The dashed boxes are where the link stickers go.
- `highlights/`: 5 highlight covers: Work, Law firms, Medical, Leads, Free redesign.
- `manifest.json`: the name, bio and link, plus every post's caption, hashtags, image descriptions and posting day.
- `POSTING-GUIDE.md`: step-by-step instructions for Claude to post it all.
- `src/build.mjs`: rebuilds every image (`node src/build.mjs`).

## How to hand it to Claude

1. Give Claude this folder (or `saysites-instagram.zip`).
2. Say: **"Follow POSTING-GUIDE.md to set up and post the @saysites Instagram."**
3. Sign in to Instagram yourself when asked. Claude shows you each post before sharing it.
4. Post the Stories and set up the highlights from the Instagram phone app. That part can't be done on the web.

## Things to know

- Calder & Vane, Brightwater, Lumen and Hale & Porter are SaySites **example designs**, not real clients. Every image and caption that shows one says so. Swap in real client work once you have permission.
- The SEO audit numbers (90/100) come from a real audit of the Hale & Porter example site.
- Nothing promises rankings or a number of leads.
