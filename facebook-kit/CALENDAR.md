# Posting order

Twelve posts, three a week, four weeks. Order matters more than dates.

The shape is deliberate: never two link posts in a row. Facebook's feed treats a
Page that only ever posts links as a Page that only ever wants something, so
each link post sits between posts that are useful on their own.

## Notes

**Set the Page up before post 1.** Name, username, cover, profile picture,
About, services and the action button are all in `CAPTIONS.md`. A first post
landing on a half-built Page wastes its best day — it is the one most likely to
send someone to look at the Page itself.

**Spacing.** Several hours between posts, not several minutes. Posting in a
burst from a browser session is a common way to trigger a temporary block.

**Scheduling is fine here**, unlike on Instagram. Meta Business Suite's planner
will hold the whole queue. Check each link post's preview card has built before
scheduling it.

**Comments.** If somebody asks a question under a post, answering is worth more
than the next post is. That is a human job — nothing in this kit answers for
you, and the brief tells the agent not to reply on your behalf.

**After the twelve.** Each service page on the site carries a four-question FAQ,
five pages' worth. Those twenty questions are already written and already
honest, and each is a post in the shape of 8, 9 and 11: question up top, answer
in the caption, link at the end. Add them to `posts.json` and re-run `build.py`.
