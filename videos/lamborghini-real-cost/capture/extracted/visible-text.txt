The real cost of owning a Lamborghini Urus SE for one year.
Sticker: $250,490 (MSRP $246,495 + $3,995 destination).
Sales tax ~7% (varies by state): ~$17,500.
Depreciation: iSeeCars — Urus loses 34.2% over five years → average ~$17,100 per year.
Insurance: ~$6,600/yr (Urus average $6,635/yr, Insuranceopedia/InsuredBetter).
Annual dealer service: ~$2,000 ($1,500–$2,500).
Tires: ~$3,000 per set (23-inch performance tires; $2,000–$5,000 range).
Opportunity cost: $250,000 earning 5% elsewhere ≈ $12,500/yr.
Year-one total on top of the car ≈ $58,700 ≈ $4,900 per month.
Message: looking rich and being rich are different math.
