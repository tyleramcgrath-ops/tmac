## Line 1 — What they see (Frame 1)

    Everyone sees the private jet. The champagne. The lifestyle.

## Line 2 — The price (Frame 2)

    Nobody sees the bill. Nine and a half million dollars, just to buy one.

## Line 3 — Fixed costs (Frame 3)

    Pilots, hangar, insurance, management. Three hundred sixty grand a year, before it even flies.

## Line 4 — Flying it (Frame 4)

    Fly it a hundred hours? Another hundred sixty-five grand.

## Line 5 — Opportunity cost (Frame 5)

    And that nine and a half million, invested at five percent? Four hundred seventy grand a year. Gone.

## Line 6 — The total (Frame 6)

    That's almost a million dollars a year. Ten thousand dollars for every hour in the air.

## Line 7 — Outro (Frame 7)

    They see the jet. Now you know the math. Still want the jet?
