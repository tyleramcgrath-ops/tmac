# Apex Revival: Batch 2 Shorts

*The real economics behind luxury, wealth and status. No hype, no fake gurus.*

Research date: 2026-10-03. Every number in the narration comes from a source in that script's **Sources** list. Anything marked **(est.)** is our own arithmetic on sourced inputs, and the assumptions are listed under **Math**. Where sources give a range, the scripts use the conservative (low) end unless a line says otherwise.

Format: 8 lines per script, 70–85 words, about 28–33 s at a brisk pace. Line 1 is the hook. Line 2 is "what they see". Lines 3–6 are cost beats with one number each. Line 7 is the climax. Line 8 is the looping comment bait. Each line has an on-screen **receipt tag** (LABEL · value).

## 1. What a superyacht really costs per year

**YouTube title:** What a $30M Superyacht Really Costs Per Year *(44 chars)*

### Narration (~81 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | Owning this yacht costs about eight thousand dollars a day. | RUNNING COST · ~$8,200 / day (est.) |
| 2 (what they see) | Fifty meters. Thirty million dollars. Champagne in Saint-Tropez. | 50 m YACHT · ~$30M |
| 3 | The crew, about a dozen people: one to two million a year. | CREW · $1M–$2M / yr |
| 4 | Maintenance and repairs: seven hundred grand, minimum. | MAINTENANCE · $700K–$1.2M / yr |
| 5 | Fuel: a quarter million. Double that if you actually cruise. | FUEL · $250K–$500K / yr |
| 6 | A Riviera berth: up to three hundred fifty grand. | BERTH · $150K–$350K / yr |
| 7 (climax) | The industry rule: ten percent of the price, every year. Three million dollars. | ANNUAL TOTAL · ~$3M / yr (10% rule) |
| 8 (loop) | Ten years in, you've paid for it twice. Still want the yacht? | 10 YEARS · ~$30M (est.) |

### Math
- Purchase price $30M is an assumption at the top of Ocean Independence's 50 m pre-owned range ($10M–$30M; new build $15M–$25M).
- 10% rule: $30M × 10% = $3M/yr. Fraser says 10–15% of purchase value; Ocean Independence says 10–20%. So $3M is the low end.
- $3,000,000 ÷ 365 ≈ **$8,219/day (est.)**, spoken as "about eight thousand".
- Ten years × $3M = **$30M (est.)**, which equals the purchase price. That's where "paid for it twice" comes from.
- Cross-check: Ocean Independence's line items for a 50 m yacht total **$2.3M–$4.1M/yr**, so $3M sits inside that range.

### Description
A 50-metre superyacht runs about 10% of its price every year, in crew, maintenance, fuel and berths. Figures from Fraser Yachts and Ocean Independence (2026).
#shorts #superyacht #luxury #wealth #moneyfacts

### Sources
- Ocean Independence, "How Much Does a Superyacht Cost? (2026 Guide)," Apr 1 2026. Gives 50 m crew $1M–$2M, fuel $250K–$500K, maintenance and refit $700K–$1.2M, insurance $100K–$250K, berthing $150K–$350K, total $2.3M–$4.1M, and running costs of 10–20% of value: https://www.oceanindependence.com/articles/how-much-does-a-superyacht-cost/
- Fraser Yachts, "What does a superyacht cost to run per year?," Apr 22 2026. Gives running costs of 10–15% of purchase value, 8–15 crew, salaries of €40K to €150K+, maintenance of 5–10% of value, and insurance of 0.5–2%: https://www.fraseryachts.com/en/hidden-costs-in-yacht-ownership/
- Ocean Independence, ownership cost breakdown (context): https://www.oceanindependence.com/yacht-management/operational-yacht-management/how-much-does-it-cost-to-own-a-superyacht/

### Caveats
- Costs depend heavily on usage (how many charter or cruising weeks), flag, location and the age of the yacht. Many owners charter their yachts to offset costs. Mention this in a pinned comment if viewers push back.
- The "8K a day" hook includes fuel and maintenance. Fixed costs alone (crew, berth, insurance, at the low end) come to roughly $1.25M+/yr, or about $3,400/day (est.).
- Major refits (every 5–7 years) can add 10–20% of the yacht's value on top, so the script understates costs.

## 2. The bill after you buy a $10M mansion

**YouTube title:** The Bill After You Buy a $10M Mansion *(37 chars)*

### Narration (~84 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | Buying a ten-million-dollar mansion is the cheap part. | PRICE · $10,000,000 |
| 2 (what they see) | Los Angeles. Infinity pool, canyon views, full-time staff. | LOCATION · Los Angeles, CA |
| 3 | Property tax, about one point two percent: a hundred twenty grand a year. | PROPERTY TAX · ~$120K / yr (est.) |
| 4 | Insuring the house and everything in it: about seventy-two grand. | INSURANCE · ~$72K / yr |
| 5 | Upkeep, using the one-percent rule: another hundred grand. | MAINTENANCE · $100K+ / yr (1–4% rule) |
| 6 | A house manager, a private chef and a housekeeper: two hundred ninety grand, at the low end. | STAFF ×3 · $290K+ / yr |
| 7 (climax) | Before you flip a light switch: almost six hundred grand a year. Forty-eight thousand a month. | TOTAL · ~$582K / yr · ~$48.5K / mo (est.) |
| 8 (loop) | Still want the mansion? | STILL WANT IT? |

### Math
- Property tax: a new purchase is reassessed at the price paid. $10M × 1.2% = **$120,000 (est.)**. LA County 2025–26 effective rates run about 1.14–1.25% depending on bonds and district. Ownwell gives the City of LA median as 1.26%.
- Insurance: $71,750/yr is Coastal Insurance Solutions' estimate for a $10M home. It assumes $17.5M total insured value: $10M dwelling, $2.5M other structures, $5M contents, at $0.41 per $100. That's what "the house and everything in it" refers to.
- Maintenance: 1% × $10M = **$100,000 (est.)**. The 1–4% rule comes from State Farm and Fannie Mae; 4% would be $400K.
- Staff (low end of the Household Staff Agency 2025/26 US ranges): house manager $100K, private chef $100K, housekeeper $90K, total **$290,000**. Ranges are $100K–$180K, $100K–$300K and $90K–$160K. This excludes employer payroll taxes and benefits.
- Total: $120,000 + $71,750 + $100,000 + $290,000 = **$581,750/yr (est.)**. Divided by 12, that's **$48,479/mo (est.)**.

### Description
A $10M LA home costs almost $600K a year to run, before utilities: property tax, insurance, upkeep and staff. Sources: LA County tax rates (Ownwell), Coastal Insurance, State Farm, Household Staff Agency 2025/26.
#shorts #luxuryrealestate #mansion #wealth #moneyfacts

### Sources
- Ownwell, Los Angeles property tax (median effective rate 1.26%): https://www.ownwell.com/trends/california/los-angeles-county/los-angeles
- Search-aggregated LA County 2025–26 effective range of 1.14–1.22% (context; verify for the specific parcel): https://honestcasa.com/taxes/california/los-angeles-property-tax-guide
- Coastal Insurance Solutions, "How Much Does it Cost to Insure a Multimillion-Dollar Home?" (updated Aug 15 2025; $10M home ≈ $71,750/yr): https://coastalinsurancesolution.com/cost-to-insure-a-multimillion-dollar-home/
- State Farm, home maintenance budget (1–4% of home value): https://www.statefarm.com/simple-insights/residence/how-to-budget-and-save-for-home-maintenance
- Fannie Mae, maintenance budget (same 1–4% rule): https://yourhome.fanniemae.com/own/how-build-your-maintenance-and-repair-budget
- Household Staff Agency, Household Staff Annual Report 2025/26 (US: house manager $100K–$180K, private chef $100K–$300K, housekeeper $90K–$160K, estate manager $150K–$250K): https://www.householdstaff.agency/household-staff-annual-report/

### Caveats
- The **insurance figure is from a New York (Long Island) agency's model**, not an LA quote. After the 2025 LA wildfires, many LA high-value homes are on the FAIR Plan or surplus lines, and premiums can be far higher. Treat $72K as a conservative placeholder.
- The 1% maintenance rule is applied to the full $10M price, but much of an LA price is land. A strict reader could argue for less, though luxury systems (pools, smart-home tech, landscaping) usually push costs up.
- Utilities are deliberately excluded ("before you flip a light switch"). GOBankingRates (Apr 2024) reports mega-mansion bills of $10K–$50K+/month, but those homes are much bigger than a typical $10M house, so we left them out.
- One-time costs such as LA's Measure ULA transfer tax are excluded because the script is about the annual bill.

## 3. What a VIP club table really costs

**YouTube title:** What a Vegas VIP Table Really Costs *(35 chars)*

### Narration (~82 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | A Vegas club will sell you vodka for seven hundred fifty dollars. | GREY GOOSE · up to $750 |
| 2 (what they see) | Sparklers, a booth above the crowd, the bottle parade. | VIP TABLE · XS / OMNIA tier |
| 3 | Weekend minimums at the top clubs: three to six grand. | WEEKEND MINIMUM · $3K–$6K |
| 4 | Two Grey Goose, one Dom Pérignon. That's your three grand. | BOTTLES · 2×$750 + 1×$1,500 = $3,000 |
| 5 | Then tax, venue fee and auto-gratuity: up to thirty-eight percent on top. | TAX + FEE + TIP · +34–38% |
| 6 | Your three-grand table? Over four thousand dollars. | ALL-IN · ~$4,080 (est.) |
| 7 (climax) | The same three bottles at a liquor store: about three hundred bucks. | RETAIL VALUE · ~$307 (est.) |
| 8 (loop) | Thirteen times the price. Would you still book the table? | MARKUP · ~13× (est.) |

### Math
- Bottle prices at premium clubs (XS, OMNIA) from NoCoverVegas 2026: Grey Goose $650–$750 and Dom Pérignon $1,000–$1,500. The script uses the **top** of each range so the bottles add up to a clean $3,000 minimum: 2 × $750 + $1,500 = $3,000. If a lower-end price is preferred, use 2 × $650 + $1,000 = $2,300 and adjust line 4.
- Tax, fees and tip: Discotech says Las Vegas runs 34–38% on top of the minimum. At the 36% midpoint, $3,000 × 1.36 = **$4,080 (est.)**. The range is $4,020–$4,140, so "over four thousand" holds either way.
- Retail: Grey Goose averages $29 per 750 ml (Wine-Searcher US average, ex-tax), and Dom Pérignon 2017 is $248.97 at Total Wine. 2 × $29 + $248.97 = **$306.97 (est.)**.
- Markup: $4,080 ÷ $307 ≈ **13.3× (est.)**.

### Description
Vegas bottle service, line by line: premium-club bottle prices, the 34–38% in tax, fees and auto-tip, and what the same bottles cost retail. Sources: NoCoverVegas, Discotech, Wine-Searcher, Total Wine (2026).

### Sources
- NoCoverVegas, "Las Vegas Bottle Service 2026: Prices" (premium-club bottle table; XS minimums from $1,500; Nevada sales tax 8.375%; 18–20% service charge; 8–15% venue fee): https://nocovervegas.com/guides/bottle-service
- Vinny.Vegas, "Las Vegas Bottle Service Prices: 2026" (premium XS/Omnia weekend tables from about $3,000–$6,000), Apr 29 2026: https://vinny.vegas/guides/las-vegas-bottle-service-prices/
- Discotech Help Center, tax and gratuity for bottle service (Las Vegas 34–38% on top; NYC and Miami 28–34%): https://help.discotech.me/articles/what-is-the-tax-and-gratuity-for-bottle-service/
- LasVegasNightclubs.com (sales tax 8.375%, venue fee 12.75%, gratuity 20%): https://lasvegasnightclubs.com/las-vegas-nightclub-bottle-service/

- Total Wine, Dom Pérignon Brut 2017 ($248.97): https://www.totalwine.com/wine/champagne-sparkling-wine/champagne/brut/dom-perignon-brut-champagne/p/226518750

### Caveats
- Club prices come from **promoter and guide sites, not official club menus**. XS and Omnia don't publish current menus. Prices change by night, season and headliner.
- Club vodka is often a **1-litre** bottle; retail is quoted per 750 ml. A 1 L Grey Goose costs a bit more at retail, but the markup is still about 12–13×.
- The minimum is spent on bottles, so the table, mixers, the booth and service are what you're really paying for. Say this fairly and don't call it a "scam".
- Some clubs (for example, XS on certain promo nights, per one promoter) quote lower fees. The 34–38% figure is the typical range, not a universal one.

## 4. What it costs to look rich on Instagram

**YouTube title:** What It Costs to Look Rich on Instagram *(39 chars)*

### Narration (~83 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | That private-jet photo? It might be a studio in East LA. | "JET" STUDIO · East Los Angeles |
| 2 (what they see) | The Lambo, the mansion, the jet, the Birkin. Rich, right? | THE FEED · Lambo · Mansion · Jet · Birkin |
| 3 | The Lamborghini: rentals in LA start around a thousand a day. | LAMBO RENTAL · ~$1,000 / day |
| 4 | The mansion: listed by the hour. Four hours, about eight hundred. | MANSION SET · $200/hr × 4 = $800 |
| 5 | The jet set: around forty-five dollars an hour. | JET STUDIO · $35–$55 / hr |
| 6 | The Birkin: a rental membership, eight hundred a month. | BIRKIN RENTAL · $800 / mo |
| 7 (climax) | One rich-looking photo day: about twenty-eight hundred dollars. Owned at the end: nothing. | PHOTO DAY · ~$2,780 (est.) · OWNED · $0 |
| 8 (loop) | Next time you see the jet pic: real, or rented? | REAL or RENTED? |

### Math
- Lamborghini: Pugachev Company (LA) lists "starting at $1,000 daily". Its Huracán EVO starts at $660/day, and other LA renters list $999–$1,199 for a Huracán Spyder or EVO. The script uses **$1,000**.
- Mansion: Peerspace's Bel Air Tropical Mansion is $200/hr with a 2-hour minimum. Assuming a 4-hour shoot: **$800 (est.)**.
- Jet studio: FD Photo Studio (Olympic 4, East LA) is $34.99–$54.99/hr. Assuming 4 hours at $44.99: **$179.96 (est.)**.
- Birkin: Vivrelle Privée costs $800/month (Fashionista, Apr 30 2026).
- Total: $1,000 + $800 + $180 + $800 = **$2,780 (est.)**.

### Description
The Lambo, the mansion, the jet and the Birkin can all be rented in LA by the day, the hour or the month. Real prices from rental companies, Peerspace, FD Photo Studio and Vivrelle (via Fashionista).

### Caveats
- **Keep it non-mocking.** Don't show or name any real creator's photos. Use stock or our own B-roll of studios and rentals. The hook says "it *might* be a studio" on purpose.
- The mansion figure uses the **cheapest** listed option. An ocean-view estate ($750/hr × 6-hr minimum = $4,500) would push the day past $6,400 (est.).
- Vivrelle Privée is **invite-only**, and $800 is a monthly membership, not a one-day rental. Other services quote Birkins by the day or week (for example, ByRotation from about £150/day), but we didn't verify those.
- Excluded: photographer, insurance and deposit on the Lambo, mileage limits, and the designer outfit. Adding Rent the Runway single rentals (about $35+) barely changes the total. The true cost of the day is higher than $2,780.

## Figures to double-check before recording
1. **Mansion insurance ($71,750):** this is an NY agency's model, not an LA quote. Post-wildfire LA premiums could be much higher.
2. **Vegas bottle prices:** these come from promoter and guide sites, not official XS or Omnia menus, and bottle size (750 ml vs 1 L) isn't stated.
3. **$30M yacht price:** this is our assumption, at the top of Ocean Independence's 50 m pre-owned range. A new 50 m build is quoted at $15M–$25M.
4. **Lambo "about a thousand a day":** the same company also lists a Huracán EVO from $660/day, so ~$1,000 is a typical starting rate, not a floor.
