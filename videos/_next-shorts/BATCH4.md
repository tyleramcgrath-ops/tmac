# Apex Revival: Batch 4 Shorts

*The real economics behind luxury, wealth and status. No hype, no fake gurus.*

Research date: 2026-10-04. Every number in the narration comes from a source in that script's **Sources** list. Anything marked **(est.)** is our own arithmetic on sourced inputs, and the assumptions are listed under **Math**. Where sources give a range, the scripts use the conservative (low) end unless a line says otherwise. Script 1 is narrated in euros because every Monaco source prices in euros; the dollar conversion uses the ECB reference rate of 1 EUR = 1.1225 USD (2026-10-02).

Format: 8 lines per script, 75–85 words, about 28–33 s at a brisk pace. Line 1 is the hook. Line 2 is "what they see". Lines 3–6 are cost beats with one number each. Line 7 is the climax. Line 8 is the looping comment bait. Each line has an on-screen **receipt tag** (LABEL · value).

### Math
- Rental: Villas For Kings' Aspen guide (updated May 2026) lists **Alpine Snow Chalet, Snowmass** (Cuvée Aspen): 5 bedrooms, sleeps 10, Christmas rate **$72,000–$98,000 per week, 14-night minimum**. The script uses the low end.
- Minimum: "The booking covers the full two weeks at the listed weekly rate (multiplied by two), regardless of arrival date." $72,000 × 2 = **$144,000 (est.)**. Per night: $72,000 ÷ 7 = $10,286 (est.).
- Lift tickets: Aspen Snowmass same-day adult ticket between the Friday after Christmas and New Year's Day: **$279** (POWDER, Dec 22 2025). 10 skiers × 7 days × $279 = **$19,530 (est.)**, spoken as "about nineteen and a half thousand".
- Chef: the same guide puts chef-on-demand at **$850 to $2,400 per day plus food at cost**. Low end: 14 days × $850 = **$11,900 (est.)**, spoken as "about twelve thousand, plus food".
- Climax: $144,000 + $19,530 + $11,900 = **$175,430 (est.)**, spoken as "about a hundred seventy-five thousand".
- Per guest: $175,430 ÷ 10 = **$17,543 (est.)**, spoken as "seventeen thousand five hundred".
- Left out (that's what "before tips and tax" covers): service charge of 8–12% of rent, staff gratuities of $300–$800 per staff member per week, Colorado lodging tax, and groceries.

### Description
In Aspen, holiday rentals come with a 14-night minimum. A 5-bedroom Snowmass chalet at $72K a week becomes a $144K booking. Add a week of holiday lift tickets for ten ($279 a day) and a private chef, and it's about $175K before tips and tax. Sources: Villas For Kings (May 2026), POWDER.
