# Apex Revival: Batch 3 Shorts

*The real economics behind luxury, wealth and status. No hype, no fake gurus.*

Research date: 2026-10-03. Every number in the narration comes from a source in that script's **Sources** list. Anything marked **(est.)** is our own arithmetic on sourced inputs, and the assumptions are listed under **Math**. Where sources give a range, the scripts use the conservative (low) end unless a line says otherwise. Live fares and hotel rates (scripts 3 and 5) were pulled from Expedia on 2026-10-03 for travel dates in November 2026; re-check them the day before recording.

Format: 8 lines per script, 75–85 words, about 28–33 s at a brisk pace. Line 1 is the hook. Line 2 is "what they see". Lines 3–6 are cost beats with one number each. Line 7 is the climax. Line 8 is the looping comment bait. Each line has an on-screen **receipt tag** (LABEL · value).

## 1. What it costs to own a private island

**YouTube title:** What It Really Costs to Own a Private Island *(44 chars)*

### Narration (~82 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | A Bahamas island costs about five million. That's the easy part. | ROBERTS CAY · $5,250,000 |
| 2 (what they see) | Twelve acres in the Exumas. Two white-sand beaches. Your own harbor. | 12 ACRES · Exuma Cays, Bahamas |
| 3 | Add a house, and you need a live-in caretaker: forty-two grand a year. | CARETAKER · $42K / yr |
| 4 | Insurance in hurricane country: thirty-five thousand. | INSURANCE · $35K / yr |
| 5 | Salt air eats everything. Maintenance: fifty-five grand. | MAINTENANCE · $55K / yr |
| 6 | The boat is your only road. Fifteen thousand to run it. | BOAT · $15K / yr |
| 7 (climax) | Add solar, desalination and property tax: a hundred seventy-seven thousand a year. | POWER + WATER + TAX · $30K → TOTAL · $177K / yr |
| 8 (loop) | That's almost five hundred dollars a day. Still want the island? | PER DAY · ~$485 (est.) |

### Math
- Purchase price: Roberts Cay, 12 acres, northern Exumas, listed at **$5,250,000** on Private Islands Online. Spoken as "about five million".
- Running costs are PrivateIslandMarket's 2026 worked example for a **10-acre Exuma Cays island with a 3-bedroom home, dock, 15 kW solar and desalination**, visited 6–8 times a year: property tax $18,000 + insurance $35,000 + maintenance $55,000 + full-time live-on caretaker $42,000 + boat $15,000 + utilities (solar, desal, generator) $12,000 = **$177,000/yr**.
- Line 7's "solar, desalination and property tax" = $12,000 + $18,000 = $30,000. Lines 3–6 add up to $147,000, and $147,000 + $30,000 = $177,000.
- Per day: $177,000 ÷ 365 = **$484.93 (est.)**, spoken as "almost five hundred".
- Cross-check: the same guide puts typical annual costs at $84K–$310K, and Kepri Estates gives Caribbean staff at $2,000–$3,500 per employee per month ($24K–$42K/yr), so $42K sits at the top of that band.

### Description
A $5M Bahamas island is the cheap part. A modest developed island runs about $177K a year in caretaker, insurance, maintenance, boat, power and water. Sources: Private Islands Online listing, PrivateIslandMarket 2026 cost guide, Kepri Estates.
#shorts #privateisland #bahamas #luxury #moneyfacts

### Sources
- Private Islands Online (Private Islands Inc.), Roberts Cay listing: USD $5,250,000, 12 acres, undeveloped with existing foundations, about 28–30 miles from Nassau: https://www.privateislandsonline.com/caribbean/bahamas/the-exumas/roberts-cay
- Private Islands Online, Bahamas listings (Roberts Cay among $4.3M–$9M Bahamas islands): https://www.privateislandsonline.com/region/bahamas?page=2
- PrivateIslandMarket, "Private Island Annual Costs: What Owners Actually Spend (2026)," Apr 1 2026. Bahamas example $177K/yr with the line items above; typical range $84K–$310K/yr; developed islands with staff $500K+: https://www.privateislandmarket.com/guides/private-island-annual-costs
- Kepri Estates, "Private Island Ongoing Ownership Costs" (c. Dec 2025): Caribbean staff $2,000–$3,500/month per employee; generator fuel $1,000–$5,000/month; desalination maintenance about $15,000/yr: https://kepriestates.com/buying-private-islands-and-beaches/finance/planning/ongoing-costs/
- Context (older, 2023): Chris Krolow of Private Islands Inc. told The Globe and Mail that a Bahamas live-in caretaker costs about US$80,000, and that one owner of an island listed at $10M spent about $800,000 a year, "most of that was diesel fuel and full-time staff": https://www.theglobeandmail.com/real-estate/article-social-medias-millenial-moron-skewers-the-absurdity-of-canadian-real/

### Caveats
- **The listing and the cost model are different islands.** Roberts Cay is undeveloped (foundations only). The $177K figure is a guide's model for a similar-size Exuma island *with* a house. That's why line 3 says "Add a house". Building the house is extra and isn't counted.
- PrivateIslandMarket is a listings and guide site, not an independent research body. Its numbers are modeled examples, not audited owner costs.
- $177K is the **modest** case. The same guide says developed islands with staff run $500K+, and Krolow's (2023) example owner spent about $800K a year. Krolow's $80K caretaker figure is almost double the $42K used here. A good pinned comment: "This is the cheap version."
- The guide also says Bahamas insurance can reach 2–5% of insured value. On a large home that is well above $35K.

## 2. What it really costs to buy a Birkin

**YouTube title:** What a Birkin Really Costs (It's Not $13,500) *(45 chars)*

### Narration (~85 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | You can't just walk in and buy a Birkin. | BIRKIN · "not available" |
| 2 (what they see) | A Birkin twenty-five, in Togo leather. Straight from the boutique. | BIRKIN 25 · Togo |
| 3 | The US sticker price: thirteen and a half grand. | RETAIL (US, 2026) · $13,500 |
| 4 | First, buyers say, you build a purchase history. Scarves, shoes, home goods. | PURCHASE HISTORY · reportedly expected |
| 5 | Collectors describe spending one to one. Some say double. | SPEND RATIO · 1:1 to 1:2 (reported, unconfirmed) |
| 6 | A twenty twenty-four lawsuit alleged illegal tying. Dismissed, now on appeal. | LAWSUIT · dismissed Sept 2025 · appeal pending |
| 7 (climax) | At one to one, your thirteen-grand bag really costs twenty-seven thousand. | REAL COST · ~$27,000 (est.) |
| 8 (loop) | Or skip the line: resale runs close to thirty grand. Which would you pay? | RESALE · $28K–$30K |

### Math
- Retail: Birkin 25 in Togo is **$13,500** in the US after the January 2026 increase (Sotheby's; PurseBop). It was $12,100 in 2025, per Sotheby's.
- Climax at a 1:1 spend ratio: $13,500 of other Hermès purchases + $13,500 bag = **$27,000 (est.)**. At the 1:2 ratio collectors also describe, it's $13,500 × 2 + $13,500 = $40,500 (est.). Sales tax is excluded.
- Resale: Sotheby's says pristine Togo Birkin 25 and 30 bags "trade around $28,000 to $30,000" on its marketplace. That's about 2.1–2.2× retail (est.).

### Description
Retail on a Birkin 25 is $13,500, but buyers report needing a purchase history first. Hermès has never confirmed a spend ratio, and a 2024 lawsuit over the practice was dismissed and is on appeal. Sources: Sotheby's, PurseBop, NBC News, The Fashion Law.
#shorts #hermes #birkin #luxury #moneyfacts

### Sources

- PurseBop, "Hermès Birkin Bag Price Guide 2026," Mar 28 2026 (B25 $13,500, B30 $14,900, B35 $16,300): https://www.pursebop.com/the-hermes-birkin-price-guide-2026/
- PurseBop, "Can You Explain the Concept of the 'Hermès Spend Ratio'?" (ratios of "1:1 or 1:1.5 or 1:2"; "Hermès has never publicly stated that there is a spend ratio"): https://www.pursebop.com/dearpursebop/question/can-you-explain-the-concept-and-terms-of-the-hermes-spend-ratio
- NBC News, "Hermès lawsuit claims luxury retailer reserves its famed Birkin bags only for its biggest spenders," Mar 21 2024 (alleges associates only offer Birkins to clients with a "sufficient 'purchase history'"; 3% commission on ancillary items): https://www.nbcnews.com/business/hermes-lawsuit-claims-luxury-retailer-reserves-famed-birkin-bags-only-rcna144480

### Caveats
- **Attribute everything about the "spend ratio."** It comes from customers and forums. Hermès has never confirmed it, and the lawsuit doesn't name a ratio. Keep the words "buyers say", "collectors describe" and "alleged" in the VO, and add an on-screen "reported, unconfirmed" tag.
- **The lawsuit was dismissed** (with prejudice, Sept 17 2025, N.D. Cal.) because the plaintiffs failed to plead a market and market power. The appeal was pending as of May 2026. Don't imply Hermès was found liable. Re-check the Ninth Circuit status before posting.
- The "pre-spend" buys real goods (scarves, shoes) that keep some value, so $27K isn't $27K thrown away. That's a fair pushback, and it fits a pinned comment.
- If anyone asks: PurseBop's 2025 price history shows a different 2025 figure ($12,700 in May 2025) than Sotheby's ($12,100). The narration only uses the 2026 price, which both agree on.

## 3. What a first-class suite really costs

| 6 | First: about fourteen hundred fifty an hour. | FIRST · ~$1,449 / hr (est.) |
| 7 (climax) | That's twenty-three economy tickets for one suite. | 1 SUITE = ~23 ECONOMY SEATS (est.) |
| 8 (loop) | And the shower? Five minutes of water. Worth eighteen grand? | SHOWER · 5 min of water |

### Math
- Fares are from an Expedia search run 2026-10-03 for **EK204, JFK → DXB nonstop, Wed Nov 4 2026, one adult, one-way**, scheduled at 12h 30m:
  - First (First Flex, refundable): **$18,116.90** total ($17,277 base + $839.90 taxes).
  - Economy (Economy Flex): **$772.90** total ($565 base + $207.90 taxes).
- Per hour: $18,116.90 ÷ 12.5 = **$1,449.35/hr (est.)**; $772.90 ÷ 12.5 = **$61.83/hr (est.)**.
- Ratio: $18,116.90 ÷ $772.90 = **23.4× (est.)**, spoken as "twenty-three economy tickets".
- Cross-check: Kiwi.com showed the same EK204 economy at $821 one-way for that date, which would make the ratio about 22×. "Twenty-three" uses the like-for-like Expedia pair.

### Description
Emirates First, New York to Dubai, one-way: $18,117. Economy on the same flight: $773. That's about $1,450 an hour vs $62 an hour. Fares from an Expedia search for a Nov 2026 flight; shower details from Simple Flying.

### Sources
- Expedia flight search, JFK → DXB, Emirates EK204, Nov 4 2026, run 2026-10-03 (First Flex $18,116.90; Economy Flex $772.90; 12h 30m nonstop). Live search, so screen-record or screenshot it as the receipt: https://www.expedia.com/Flights

- Simple Flying, "The Only 2 Airlines That Still Offer Onboard Shower Suites In 2026," Jun 26 2026 ("up to five minutes of running water"; one 30-minute reservation per flight): https://simpleflying.com/airlines-still-offer-onboard-shower-suites-2026/

- **Fares are live and change daily.** Both are flexible, refundable fare types, so they aren't the cheapest saver fares. Many first-class flyers use miles or upgrades and never pay cash. Say "cash fare" in a pinned comment.

- The shower is the A380 product. EK204 was an A380 in our sources, but Emirates can swap aircraft. If the flight is flown by a 777, there's no shower.
- Line 4 says "same plane": the economy and first fares were quoted for the same flight number and departure time (EK204, 10:40 AM).

## 4. What courtside NBA seats really cost

**YouTube title:** What Lakers Courtside Seats Cost for a Season *(45 chars)*

### Narration (~84 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | Lakers courtside costs at least four grand. Per seat. Per night. | COURTSIDE · from $4,000 / seat |
| 2 (what they see) | Your sneakers on the baseline. Celebrities one chair over. | ROW AA · Crypto.com Arena |
| 3 | A regular-season night: four to ten thousand dollars a seat. | REG. SEASON · $4K–$10K / seat |

| 7 (climax) | Two seats, every home game, cheapest nights: about three hundred thirty grand. | SEASON · 2 SEATS · ~$328K (est.) |
| 8 (loop) | Before a single playoff game. Would you sit courtside? | + PLAYOFFS · not included |

### Math
- Per-seat regular-season price: **$4,000–$10,000** (New York Post, reported via Yahoo Sports, Apr 9 2026). The script uses the **low end**.
- One seat, full home season: 41 × $4,000 = **$164,000 (est.)**. An NBA team plays 41 of its 82 games at home.
- Two seats: 2 × $164,000 = **$328,000 (est.)**, spoken as "about three hundred thirty grand".
- At the top of the range: 2 × 41 × $10,000 = $820,000 (est.).
- Playoffs: $20,000–$40,000 per seat (same NY Post report). Courtside Reserve: a team source said 78% of standard courtside playoff prices, which the Post put at about $15,000–$30,000 per seat.

### Description
Lakers courtside runs $4K–$10K a seat for a regular-season game, per the New York Post. Two seats for all 41 home games, at the cheapest prices, comes to about $328K, before any playoff games.

### Sources
- Yahoo Sports (New York Post), "Courtside tickets for Lakers game typically cost…," Apr 9 2026 ("between $4,000-$10,000 per seat depending on the day of the week and the matchup. For the postseason, that cost can range from $20,000-$40,000"): https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/courtside-tickets-lakers-game-typically-192050329.html
- Yahoo Sports (The California Post / NY Post), "Shocking price tag for Lakers' new playoff courtside seats revealed," Apr 9 2026 (Courtside Reserve at 78% of courtside playoff pricing, about $15K–$30K; includes in-seat food and drink and VIP club access): https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/shocking-price-tag-lakers-playoff-231412661.html
- Context: TickPick, "How Much are NBA Courtside Tickets?" (league-wide ranges): https://www.tickpick.com/blog/how-much-are-nba-courtside-tickets/

### Caveats
- **These are market (resale) prices, not the Lakers' face-value season-ticket prices**, which aren't public. Most courtside seats belong to long-time season-ticket holders. Someone who holds them at face value may pay less than resale. That's the most likely pushback, so put "resale market" in the on-screen tag.
- $4,000 is the floor of a reported range, so "at least" and "cheapest nights" are doing honest work. Real-world prices for marquee games (Celtics, Clippers) are well above it.
- Extras like parking and food are left out. Courtside packages often include in-seat service and club access, so we didn't add them.
- The figures are from the 2025–26 season reporting. 2026–27 prices may differ.

## 5. What it costs to live in a 5-star hotel

**YouTube title:** What It Costs to Live at The Plaza Hotel *(40 chars)*

### Narration (~76 words)

| # | Spoken line | Receipt tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (hook) | You can actually live at the Plaza. Here's the bill. | THE PLAZA · New York |
| 2 (what they see) | Central Park out the window. Room service. Someone else makes the bed. | 5★ · Fifth Ave & Central Park South |
| 3 | One standard room, one November night: about seventeen hundred dollars. | 1 NIGHT · $1,696 |
| 4 | Taxes and fees push it past two thousand. | ALL-IN · $2,024 / night |
| 5 | Book twenty-eight nights: sixty-six thousand dollars. | 28 NIGHTS · $66,366 |
| 6 | The median Manhattan rent? Forty-nine hundred a month. | MEDIAN RENT · $4,900 / mo |
| 7 (climax) | A full year at the Plaza: over seven hundred thousand dollars. | 1 YEAR · ~$739K (est.) |
| 8 (loop) | One Plaza month covers thirteen people's rent. Would you check in? | 28 NIGHTS ÷ RENT · ~13.5× (est.) |

### Math
- Rates are from an Expedia search run 2026-10-03 for The Plaza, A Fairmont Hotel, one adult:
  - 1 night, Nov 2–3 2026: **$1,696** nightly rate; **$2,024** total with taxes and fees.
  - 28 nights, Nov 2–30 2026 (Expedia's maximum search length): **$66,366** total, which works out to $1,998/night before taxes.
- One year (conservative): $2,024 × 365 = **$738,760 (est.)**, spoken as "over seven hundred thousand". Using the 28-night average instead ($66,366 ÷ 28 = $2,370.21/night all-in) gives $865,128 (est.).
- Rent comparison: $66,366 ÷ $4,900 = **13.5 (est.)**, spoken as "thirteen people's rent".

### Description
A November night at The Plaza is about $1,700, or $2,024 after taxes and fees. Twenty-eight nights comes to $66,366, about 13 times the median Manhattan rent ($4,900, Aug 2026). Rates from an Expedia search; rent from the Miller Samuel / Real Deal report via Brick Underground.
#shorts #theplaza #newyork #luxuryhotel #moneyfacts

### Sources
- Expedia, The Plaza, A Fairmont Hotel (searched 2026-10-03; 1 night Nov 2–3 2026 = $1,696 nightly / $2,024 total; 28 nights Nov 2–30 2026 = $66,366 total): https://www.expedia.com/.h28044.Hotel-Information?chkin=2026-11-02&chkout=2026-11-30&rm1=a1
- Brick Underground, Manhattan/Brooklyn rental market report, Sep 10 2026 (Manhattan median rent $4,900 in August 2026, up 6.5% year over year; July record $5,000; via Jonathan Miller's Real Deal Rental Report): https://www.brickunderground.com/rent/nyc-manhattan-brooklyn-rental-market-report-august-2026
- NYC Department of Finance, Hotel Room Occupancy Tax (permanent residents, meaning 180+ consecutive days, are exempt): https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/business/business-hotel-room-occupancy-tax.page
- NYC DOF hotel-tax FAQ (PDF): https://www.nyc.gov/assets/finance/downloads/pdf/faq/hotel_occuupancy_tax.pdf

### Caveats
- **Hotel rates are live and seasonal.** November includes Thanksgiving week, which is likely why the 28-night average ($1,998) is higher than the single-night rate ($1,696). Re-check before recording.
- **A real year-long stay would be cheaper per night.** NYC's hotel occupancy tax stops after 180 consecutive days, and state and local sales tax stops after 90 (per NYC DOF and tax guides). Hotels also negotiate long-stay rates. That's why the climax uses the lower single-night all-in rate and says "over seven hundred thousand" rather than $865K.
- The Expedia room is the entry-level room shown. Suites cost several times more.
- Manhattan median rent varies by report: Corcoran's August 2026 figure is about $5,300. Using $4,900 makes the comparison conservative.
