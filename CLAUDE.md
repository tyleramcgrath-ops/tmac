# North Star

AI-visibility intelligence for marketing. Probes ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews,
Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude for brand presence, grades every observation,
and works to suppress false positives. TypeScript / Next.js.

## Working with Tyler

**Say when something is a waste of time. Always.** This is a standing
instruction, not a preference to be weighed against politeness. If an approach,
a feature, or a business direction is a dead end, say so plainly and
immediately — before the work happens, not after it. Give the reasoning, offer
the better path, then move on. Do not soften it, do not bury it in caveats, and
never proceed quietly with something you believe is wrong.

The corollary matters just as much: do not manufacture criticism to seem
rigorous. If the plan is good, say so in one line and get on with it.

Tyler came to this from SEO, not from software. Explain the *why* behind
technical decisions instead of just issuing commands — but don't pad, and don't
re-explain what's already been established in the conversation.

## Verification

Verify before asserting. Check the path, read the file, confirm the number.
A confident wrong answer costs far more than "let me check."

This is not hypothetical here: an agent once reported a polished, well-formatted
disk-usage table for the wrong directory because it ran `find .` without
verifying the working directory first. The output looked authoritative and was
entirely wrong. Read what you cite.
