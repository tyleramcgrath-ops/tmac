Who we are

A long-term owner and operator in communications.

NetworkIP was founded in 1998 in Longview, Texas. What began as an international voice service provider has grown into a group of specialized companies that share a common foundation: carrier-grade engineering, direct relationships with the largest U.S. networks and deep regulatory experience.

As a privately held company, we invest for the long term. We give each operating company the infrastructure and support to focus on its market, and we hold every one of them to the same standard of reliability.

PPPeter R. PattulloCo-founder & Chief Executive Officer
