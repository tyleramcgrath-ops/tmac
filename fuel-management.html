How it works

Three places fuel money leaks.

Idling, inefficient routing and transactions nobody reconciles. All three are measurable from the same device.

Idle

Five minutes a shift is a real number.

Idle is measured per driver and per vehicle, then priced. Across fifty vehicles those minutes become thousands of dollars a month — and once it is reported in dollars, it changes.

  • Per-driver idle time reported in dollars
  • Idle thresholds by vehicle class and season
  • Exception alerts on sustained idling
  • Trend reporting against your own baseline
Reconciliation

Match every gallon to a vehicle that was there.

Fuel card transactions are matched against vehicle position and tank capacity. A fill that does not line up with a vehicle at that station is flagged rather than filed.

  • Fuel card transaction matching by position and time
  • Exception flags on mismatched or oversized fills
  • Cost-per-mile benchmarking by vehicle and class
  • MPG trending that separates driver from route
Where it pays
