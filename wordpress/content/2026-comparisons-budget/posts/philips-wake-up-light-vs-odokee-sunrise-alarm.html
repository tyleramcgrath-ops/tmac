Head to head

Wake-up light

Philips lists up to 300 lux with 20 brightness levels and a 20, 30 or 40-minute sunrise. Odokee lets you set the sunrise anywhere from 5 to 60 minutes but does not list its brightness. Edge: Philips.

Sounds

Philips has 5 natural wake sounds and FM radio. Odokee has 7 alarm sounds, 25 sleep sounds with a timer and a Bluetooth speaker. Edge: Odokee.

Track record

The Philips has about 14,800 Amazon ratings and is widely seen as the benchmark. The Odokee is newer, with about 2,300. Edge: Philips.

Price

When we checked, the Philips was about $96 to $107 from other sellers, and the Odokee was about $54 to $60. Edge: Odokee.

Frequently asked questions

Do sunrise alarm clocks work?

Many people find it easier to wake up to light that slowly gets brighter, especially on dark winter mornings.

Is the Philips Wake-Up Light worth it?

If you want the most proven sunrise light with strong brightness and sunset mode, many owners say yes. If you also want sleep sounds, the Odokee costs less.

Does the Odokee have white noise?

Yes. It has 25 sleep sounds, including white, pink and brown noise, with a timer up to 4 hours.

Does the Philips Wake-Up Light need an app?

No. It works on its own with buttons and a tap snooze.
