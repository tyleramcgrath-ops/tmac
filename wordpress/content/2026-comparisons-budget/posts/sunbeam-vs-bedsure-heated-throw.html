Head to head

Feel

The Sunbeam is a thin fleece. The Bedsure is a thicker reversible throw with ribbed flannel on top and sherpa underneath, so it feels cozier even when it is switched off. Edge: Bedsure.

Controls

The Sunbeam has 4 heat settings and switches off after 4 hours. The Bedsure has 6 heat levels, a timer you can set from 1 to 10 hours and a preheat mode. Edge: Bedsure.

Warranty

Sunbeam backs its throw with a 5-year limited warranty. We could not find warranty terms on the Bedsure listing. Edge: Sunbeam.

Price

When we checked, the Sunbeam was about $33 to $40. The Bedsure was about $40 to $50 on sale, with a $68.99 list price. Edge: Sunbeam.

Frequently asked questions

Are heated throws safe?

Modern heated throws have auto shut-off and overheat protection. Follow the instructions, do not fold them while on, and do not use them with infants or anyone who cannot feel heat.

Can you wash a heated throw?

Yes, both of these are machine washable once you unplug and remove the controller. Follow the care label for drying.

How long does the Sunbeam heated throw stay on?

It shuts off automatically after 4 hours.

Does the Bedsure heated throw have a timer?

Yes. You can set it for 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 hours, and it shuts off after 8 hours by default.
