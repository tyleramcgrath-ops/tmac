Head to head

Fill and feel

Coop blends cross-cut memory foam with microfiber, which makes the fill fluffier. Royal Therapy uses shredded memory foam. Both let you remove fill to lower the pillow. Edge: tie.

Adjustability

Both are adjustable. Coop also includes an extra half pound of fill, so you can make it fuller as well as flatter. Edge: Coop.

Proof

Coop has about 64,000 Amazon ratings and has been tested by several review sites. Royal Therapy has about 7,000 ratings, and we found no hands-on professional reviews. Edge: Coop.

Warranty

Coop offers a 100-night trial and 5-year warranty when you buy from its own site. Royal Therapy lists a 30-day manufacturer warranty on Amazon. Edge: Coop.

Price

When we checked, the queen Coop was about $89 and the queen Royal Therapy was about $65. Edge: Royal Therapy.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Coop pillow worth it?

For many sleepers, yes. You can tune the height, it comes with extra fill, and it is backed by a long trial and warranty when bought direct.

Can you wash a Coop pillow?

Yes. Remove the cover, wash both cold on gentle with no softener or bleach, and tumble dry low until fully dry.

Does the Coop pillow smell?

Some reviewers noticed a smell at first. Airing it out for a day or two usually helps.

Are shredded memory foam pillows good for side sleepers?

They can be, because you can add fill until the pillow is tall enough to keep your neck level.
