Head to head

Sound

The Dohm’s sound comes from a real spinning fan, which many people find more natural. The LectroFan creates 10 fan sounds and 10 noise colors digitally, and none of them loop. Edge: tie.

Volume control

The Dohm has two speeds and you shape the sound by twisting the collar. The LectroFan has up and down buttons with fine steps, and reviewers say it gets loud. Edge: LectroFan.

Features

The Dohm has no timer. The LectroFan has a sleep timer that runs in 60-minute steps, then fades out. Edge: LectroFan.

Simplicity

The Dohm has one switch and no lights or screen. It is about as simple as a machine gets. Edge: Dohm.

Travel

The LectroFan is smaller and runs on USB, so you can even power it from a laptop. The Dohm needs a wall outlet. Edge: LectroFan.

Which should you buy?

Get the Dohm if:

  • You want the sound of a real fan
  • You want the simplest possible machine
  • You never use a timer

Get the LectroFan if:

  • You want to try different sounds, like brown or pink noise
  • You need it loud, or want fine volume control
  • You travel and want USB power

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Dohm or LectroFan?

Neither is better for everyone. The Dohm is best if you want a real fan sound and simple controls. The LectroFan is best if you want more sounds, finer volume and a timer.

Does the LectroFan have a timer?

Yes. Each press adds 60 minutes, then the sound fades out. There is no shorter setting.

Is the Dohm a real fan?

Yes. It has a fan inside that makes the sound, though it does not blow air into the room the way a regular fan does.

Can you travel with a LectroFan?

Yes. It is small and runs on USB with the included adapter, but it has no battery.
