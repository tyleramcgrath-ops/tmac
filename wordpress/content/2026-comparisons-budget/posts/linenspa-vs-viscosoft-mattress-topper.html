Head to head

Feel

Linenspa is plush and soft. ViscoSoft is denser and medium-firm, so it gives more support while still cushioning hips and shoulders. Edge: tie.

Support

Reviewers say the Linenspa can feel too soft for heavier people and stomach sleepers. The thicker, denser ViscoSoft holds up better for them. Edge: ViscoSoft.

Staying put and staying clean

Linenspa has no cover or straps, so it can shift and can only be spot cleaned. ViscoSoft has a washable cover and corner straps. Edge: ViscoSoft.

Price

When we checked, a queen Linenspa was about $48 to $60. ViscoSoft listed its queen 3-inch topper at $179.95. Edge: Linenspa.

Which should you buy?

Get the Linenspa if:

  • Your mattress feels too firm and you want a cheap fix
  • You sleep on your side or back and like a plush feel
  • It is for a guest room or a dorm

Get the ViscoSoft if:

  • You want more support, or you are a heavier sleeper
  • You hate toppers that slide around
  • You want a cover you can wash

Frequently asked questions

Is a 2-inch or 3-inch topper better?

A 2-inch topper adds softness. A 3-inch topper adds more cushioning and support, which helps if your mattress is very firm or you are a heavier sleeper.

Does the Linenspa topper have straps?

No. It has no cover or straps, so it can shift a little. A fitted sheet pulled over it helps.

Can you wash a memory foam topper?

Not the foam itself. Spot clean the foam. ViscoSoft’s cover comes off and is machine washable.

Do memory foam toppers sleep hot?

They can. Both of these use gel-infused foam, and reviewers say ViscoSoft sleeps cooler than many foam toppers.
