Head to head

Heat and cold

The RENPHO has a special head with 4 heat levels and 4 cold levels, which you can use with or without percussion. The Bob and Brad C2 has no heat or cold. Edge: RENPHO.

Power

Both list 5 speeds up to 3,200 RPM, a 10 mm amplitude and about 45 lb of stall force. Testers say the C2 is not strong enough for serious athletes. Edge: tie.

Ease of use

The C2 is lighter at about 1.5 lb and has one button. The RENPHO is heavier but has a display that shows speed, battery and temperature. Edge: tie.

Extras

The C2 comes with a carrying case. The RENPHO does not include a wall adapter. Edge: Bob and Brad C2.

Price

When we checked, the RENPHO was about $85 to $90. The C2 was on sale for about $70, and about $90 otherwise. Edge: Bob and Brad C2.

Frequently asked questions

Is a massage gun with heat worth it?

If you like warmth on sore muscles, a heated head is a nice extra. The RENPHO also has a cold setting.

Is the Bob and Brad C2 powerful?

It is strong enough for most people, but testers say serious athletes may want more power.

How long do massage gun batteries last?

RENPHO says its battery lasts 3+ hours. Bob and Brad lists a 2,000 mAh battery but no runtime.

Is a massage gun a good gift?
