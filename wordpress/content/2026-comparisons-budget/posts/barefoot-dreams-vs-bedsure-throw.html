Head to head

Feel

Barefoot Dreams is a soft, slightly stretchy knit. Owners call it buttery, and that feel is why people pay for it. Bedsure is a smooth flannel fleece. It is very soft for the money, but it feels like fleece, not a luxury knit. Edge: Barefoot Dreams.

Size

The CozyChic throw is 54 x 72 inches. The Bedsure throw we compared is 50 x 60 inches, but Bedsure also sells it in eight bigger sizes. Edge: Barefoot Dreams.

Warmth

Both are lightweight blankets. Barefoot Dreams calls its throw lightweight and all-season. Some buyers say the Bedsure throw is best layered on really cold nights. Edge: tie.

Care

Barefoot Dreams asks for a cold, gentle wash, no softener or dryer sheets, and drying flat or on low. Bedsure just says machine wash. Edge: Bedsure.

Price

This is not close. When we checked, the CozyChic throw listed at $158 and the Bedsure throw was about $13. Edge: Bedsure.

Which should you buy?

Get the Barefoot Dreams if:

Get the Bedsure if:

Frequently asked questions

Is the Barefoot Dreams blanket worth it?

If you want that famous buttery knit feel, or a gift that feels luxurious, many owners say yes. If you just want a soft, warm throw, a fleece throw like Bedsure’s costs far less.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Barefoot Dreams?

The Bedsure GentleSoft throw is a soft, highly rated fleece throw for a small fraction of the price. It feels like fleece rather than a knit, so it is an alternative, not an exact copy.

How do you wash a Barefoot Dreams throw?

Machine wash cold on gentle, then lay flat to dry or tumble dry low. Skip bleach, dryer sheets and fabric softener.

Does Barefoot Dreams shed?

Experiences vary. Some owners report shedding or pilling after washing, and others report none.
