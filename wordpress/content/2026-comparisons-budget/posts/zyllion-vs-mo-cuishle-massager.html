Head to head

Design

The Zyllion is a pillow you lean against, and a strap holds it on a chair or headrest. The Mo Cuishle wraps around your neck and shoulders, and you can also use it on your back. Edge: tie.

Control

The Zyllion has one button for heat and no speed settings. The Mo Cuishle has 3 speeds and a direction button. Edge: Mo Cuishle.

Warranty

Zyllion says you get 3 years with registration. Mo Cuishle says 1 year, plus a 30-day money-back guarantee. Edge: Zyllion.

Price

When we checked, the Zyllion was about $70 to $75 and the Mo Cuishle was about $40 to $44. Edge: Mo Cuishle.

Which should you buy?

Get the Zyllion if:

Get the Mo Cuishle if:

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Zyllion or Mo Cuishle?

It depends on where you hurt. The Zyllion pillow is great for leaning back against. The Mo Cuishle wrap is better for the neck and shoulders and costs less.

Can you use a shiatsu massager in the car?

Both come with a car adapter. Use them only as a passenger or when parked, never while driving.

How long should you use a shiatsu massager?

Both shut off on their own, after 20 minutes for the Zyllion and 15 for the Mo Cuishle. Start with short sessions.

Are shiatsu massagers a good gift?

Yes, especially for people who sit at a desk or drive a lot. Heated models are some of the most-loved comfort gifts.
