Head to head

Light blocking

Both block light very well. In Reviewed’s testing, the MZOO ranked among the best masks for light blocking, and Manta’s whole design is built around blackout. Edge: tie.

Fit

The Manta’s cups slide and angle along the strap, so you can line them up with your eyes. The MZOO’s cups are fixed, and its nose area does not suit everyone. Edge: Manta.

Side sleeping

MZOO says its thin, curved sides are made for side sleepers. Manta markets the Original for back and stomach sleepers, and Reviewed found it bulkier on the side. Edge: MZOO.

Washing

The Manta goes in the washing machine in a mesh bag. MZOO suggests hand washing, though a gentle machine cycle is allowed. Edge: Manta.

Price

The MZOO was about $16 when we checked, and the Manta was $39. Edge: MZOO.

Which should you buy?

Get the MZOO if:

  • You want great blackout for less
  • You want earplugs and a travel pouch included

Get the Manta if:

  • You sleep on your back or stomach
  • Fixed masks never line up with your eyes
  • You want to toss it in the washing machine

Frequently asked questions

Is the Manta sleep mask worth it?

If fixed masks never fit your face, the Manta’s movable cups can be worth the extra cost. For side sleepers, many will prefer a slimmer mask like the MZOO.

Is the MZOO mask good for side sleepers?

MZOO designs its mask with thin, curved sides for side sleepers. It is still a contoured mask, so it is thicker than a flat one.

Can you wash a Manta sleep mask?

Yes. Manta says to machine wash it cold in a mesh laundry bag and lay it flat to dry.

Do contoured sleep masks press on your eyes?

No. The cups curve away from your eyes, so there is room to blink and no pressure on your lashes.
