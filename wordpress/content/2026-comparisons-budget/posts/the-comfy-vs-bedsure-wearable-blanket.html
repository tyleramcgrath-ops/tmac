Head to head

Warmth and softness

Both use a soft sherpa lining and both are warm. Reviewers describe The Comfy as plush and very warm, and also heavy because of its double layer. Bedsure pairs sherpa with a flannel outside and lists a 420 GSM fabric weight on its own comparison chart. Edge: tie.

Fit

This is the biggest difference. The Comfy comes in one oversized size. A 5’5” reviewer at Reviewed found the sleeves long enough to get in the way. Bedsure sells Kids, Small, Standard and Oversize, so you can match it to the person, although buyers say it runs large. Edge: Bedsure.

Pockets

The Comfy has one giant front kangaroo pocket. Bedsure has deep side pockets instead. Pick whichever style you prefer. Edge: tie.

Price

When we checked, The Comfy was about $50 and the Bedsure Grey Oversize was about $32. Prices on both change by color and size. Edge: Bedsure.

Track record

The Comfy has about 95,000 Amazon ratings and has been reviewed hands-on by several outlets. Bedsure is well rated too, but with far fewer ratings and independent reviews. Edge: The Comfy.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Comfy worth it?

For most people who want the original, yes. It is very warm, roomy and machine washable, with about 95,000 Amazon ratings. Just know it is heavy and one size.

What is the difference between The Comfy and Bedsure?

The Comfy is one size with a big front pocket. Bedsure comes in four sizes, has side pockets and cost about $18 less when we checked.

Can you wash a wearable blanket?

Yes. The Comfy says to machine wash cold and tumble dry low on its own. Bedsure says machine wash and do not bleach.

Does The Comfy run big?

Yes, by design. It is one size made to fit most adults, and shorter people may find the sleeves long.

The bottom line

Both are excellent, cozy wearable blankets. Choose The Comfy for the original roomy fit and big pocket, or the Bedsure sherpa hoodie for a lower price and more sizes. For more ideas, see our gifts for people who are always cold.
