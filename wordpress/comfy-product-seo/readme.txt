=== Comfy Product SEO ===
Requires at least: 6.2
Tested up to: 6.8
Requires PHP: 7.4
License: GPLv2 or later

One-button search optimization for Amazon affiliate products scored with the Comfy Meter.

== What it does ==

* Product schema on every product page, with your Comfy Meter as your own review
  (rating out of 10, "What we love" as pros). This makes product pages eligible for
  product results with your rating in Google. It never uses Amazon's star ratings,
  because showing another site's reviews as your own breaks Google's rules.
* A search title (up to 60 characters) and description (up to 155) for each product,
  written from that product's own summary and Comfy Meter, saved in All in One SEO
  along with a focus keyphrase. Titles and descriptions you wrote yourself are kept.
* Alt text for each product photo.
* After a CSV import, it attaches the Featured Image from URL photo when FIFU offers
  a way to, so the bulk "Update" step may not be needed (it reports "Photo attached").
* "Featured in our guides" on each product page, linking to the guides that link to it.
  It refreshes when a guide is saved.

== How to use ==

1. Upload comfy-product-seo.zip in Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin and activate it.
2. Go to Products > Product SEO and click "Optimize all products". The results table
   shows each product's new title, description, score and how many guides feature it.
3. Purge your page cache.

New products are optimized automatically when imported or saved. You can also select
products in Products > All Products and use Bulk actions > Optimize for search.

All in One SEO's TruSEO score is calculated in its editor, so it may not change until a
product is opened there. Search engines read the saved title, description and schema.
