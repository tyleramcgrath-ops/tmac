=== Comfy Humans Cloud ===
Requires at least: 6.3
Tested up to: 6.6
Requires PHP: 7.4
License: GPLv2 or later

A sky-blue WooCommerce theme for comfyhumans.com: drifting clouds, a comfort
forecast, a "why we're the comfiest" section and a mood-based product picker.

== Install ==

1. In WordPress go to Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme and upload
   comfy-humans-cloud.zip. Activate it.
2. Appearance > Customize > Comfy home page lets you change the headline, the
   intro, the hero picture, the "why we're the comfiest" reasons, the moods and
   the newsletter form. Only list reasons that are true for your shop; leave a
   reason's title empty to hide it.
3. Moods ("Mentally in bed", "A little squishy", "Maximum potato") show products
   from the product categories with the slugs mentally-in-bed, a-little-squishy
   and maximum-potato. Create those categories in Products > Categories (or
   change the slugs in the Customizer). Moods with no products are hidden.
4. Newsletter: paste a form shortcode (MailPoet, Mailchimp for WP and so on) in
   Customize > Comfy home page > Closing and newsletter. With no shortcode the
   closing section shows a shop button instead.
5. Menus: assign a menu to "Top menu" and "Footer menu" in Appearance > Menus.
   With no top menu the theme shows Shop the fluff / Why so comfy? / The Sunday
   Letter.

The home page shows up to 12 published products, most popular first, with
"Add to bag" buttons that update the bag count in the header without a reload.
With no products it shows "A little comfort is on its way."

Clouds respect the visitor's reduced-motion setting.
