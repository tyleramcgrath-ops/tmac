How it works

Video that does something with what it sees.

A camera that only records is an archive. EnVue cameras classify the event, attach the context and route it to whoever needs to act.

Evidence

The clip that settles the argument.

When an event fires, the camera saves eight seconds either side and tags it automatically. A reviewer sees the cause — a cut-in, not a distracted driver — in about a minute.

  • Automatic event-triggered capture on every hard event
  • Road-facing and optional driver-facing configurations
  • Footage tied to GPS position, speed and time
  • Clips exportable for insurers and legal teams
Prevention

Alerts in the cab, before it becomes an incident.

Distraction and following-distance warnings fire in real time for the driver. The same event lands in a coaching queue with the context attached, so review is about the situation rather than the score.

  • Real-time in-cab distraction and fatigue alerts
  • Automated driver scorecards and coaching queues
  • Trend reporting by driver, terminal and vehicle class
  • Coaching workflows that document the follow-up
Deployment

Recording the same day it is fitted.

01 — Install and calibrate

Mounted and aimed in under an hour per vehicle, with the driver-facing lens enabled or disabled to match your policy.

02 — Events classify themselves

Hard braking, swerving, distraction and following distance are detected on-device, so only real events consume review time.

03 — Coaching closes the loop

Clips land in a queue with driver, route and severity attached, and the follow-up is recorded against the event.
