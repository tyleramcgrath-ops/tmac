For our founding broker

Forty years in Palm Beach. The next way homes sell.

I want to build a marketplace where owners sell directly and buyers bid in the open, with a Florida brokerage making sure every sale is done right. I'd like you to be the founding broker: the license, the judgment and the reputation behind it.

This isn't a fight with agents. It's a better way to sell, and it needs a broker people trust to lead it.

Why it fits you

  • Open bidding ends blind offers. Sellers see what the market will truly pay, and buyers see where they stand.
  • Your expertise becomes the premium option. The platform opens doors and handles paperwork. You and the agents you pick do the advising.
  • Estates, trusts and condos are your world. Fiduciaries need a documented price. Condo buyers after Surfside want the milestone report and reserve study up front.
  • Northern and seasonal buyers can bid with confidence from 3D tours, livestreams and full documents.

Not your job: software, marketing campaigns or tech support.

How we protect your license

  1. A Florida real estate attorney reviews everything before launch, including whether auctioneer licensing applies.
  2. Deposits never touch your trust account. Bid deposits are card holds, and earnest money goes to a title company you choose.
  3. Nothing goes live, closes or wires without your approval in the console.
  4. Flat fees, paid up front, disclosed in writing. No referral payments.
  5. Fair housing built in: public Q&A, no demographic data, careful ad targeting.
  6. E&O, cyber and crime insurance in place before the first listing.

Questions you'll probably ask

"You're asking a Realtor to help replace Realtors."

I'm asking you to help change what an agent is for. Buyers can still bring an agent. Nobody is forced to pay for door-opening, and your judgment becomes what people choose to pay for.

"Will other agents be upset?"

Some, at first. We use the MLS in year one, follow its rules, and welcome agents who bring buyers. A respected broker leading it is hard to dismiss.

"Does anyone want to buy a house at auction?"

Verified buying power, the inspection before bidding, a financing window, association approval in the contract, and a backup buyer. We prove it with 10 launch homes first.

"How much of my time?"

A few hours a week during setup, then console reviews designed to take minutes. Guided-sale work is up to you.

Investor brief

Open bidding for homes, without the 6%.

Owners list for a flat fee. Verified buyers tour on their own and bid live. A licensed Florida brokerage, led by a broker with 40 years in Palm Beach County, runs every sale end to end.

The problem

  • Selling a home costs 5–6% of its price, usually the biggest fee a household ever pays.
  • Offers are blind, so sellers never learn what the market would really pay.
  • Buyers already do most of the search themselves, yet still need an agent to open the door.
  • The 2024 NAR settlement made commissions visible. Buyers now sign agreements to pay agents directly.

The product

  1. Flat-fee listing, not a percentage.
  2. See everything yourself: 3D, floor plan, livestream open house, smart-lock self-tours, and a data room with the inspection before you bid.
  3. Open, verified bidding: proxy bids, soft close, hidden reserve, buying power capped by verified funds, shill bids blocked, backup buyer.
  4. Built-in closing: e-signed contract, title and escrow tracked step by step, broker supervision throughout.

Why Palm Beach County first

  • A large market: 39 cities and towns at every price point.
  • Seasonal and out-of-state buyers who already want to buy remotely.
  • Estates, trusts and downsizers who need a transparent, defensible price.
  • Post-Surfside condo laws make document transparency a selling point.
  • Florida law fits: disclosure state, no attorney needed at closing, remote notarization, transaction brokers.
  • Our founding broker's 40-year network brings launch sellers, vendors and credibility.
