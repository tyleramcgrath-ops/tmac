# Gavel Homes: Investor Brief

**Open bidding for homes, without the 6%.** Owners list for a flat fee. Verified buyers tour on their own and bid live. A licensed Florida brokerage, led by a broker with 40 years in Palm Beach County, runs every sale end to end.

*This brief covers the opportunity, product and plan. Financial terms will be discussed separately.*

---

## The problem
- **Selling a home costs 5–6% of its price**, usually the largest single fee a household ever pays. Sellers pay it whether the home sells in a day or a year.
- **Price discovery is blind.** Offers are private and filtered through agents. Sellers never learn what the market would really pay, and buyers lose bidding wars they never saw.
- **Buyers already do most of the work** (search, research, first tours online), yet still need an agent to unlock the door and handle the paperwork.
- **The 2024 NAR settlement made commissions visible.** Buyers now sign agreements to pay agents directly. The fee is on the table, and people are questioning it.

## The solution
1. **Flat-fee listing.** No percentage of the sale.
2. **See everything yourself:** 3D walkthrough, auto floor plan, live-streamed owner open house, ID-verified self-tours with one-time smart-lock codes, and a data room with the inspection, disclosures, insurance reports and condo documents *before* you bid.
3. **Open, verified bidding:** proxy bids, soft close, hidden reserve, Buy Now, and buying power capped by verified pre-approval or proof of funds. Shill bids are blocked. A backup buyer steps in if the winner defaults.
4. **Built-in closing:** the winning bid becomes an e-signed contract, with escrow and title tracked step by step, and a licensed broker supervising throughout.

## Why it works now
- The settlement changed buyer behavior and broke the old commission default.
- Phone LiDAR, identity and funds verification, smart locks, e-signing and remote notarization are all off-the-shelf.
- AI can answer buyer questions from a listing's own documents, which is the information an agent used to relay.
- Open bidding already works elsewhere: collector cars online, eBay, and the Australian housing market.

## Why Palm Beach County first
- A large, high-turnover market with 39 cities and towns and every price point.
- Many seasonal and out-of-state buyers who already want to buy remotely.
- Equity-rich owners, estates and trusts that need a transparent, defensible price.
- Florida's post-Surfside condo laws make document transparency a selling point.
- Florida law fits: disclosure state, no attorney required at closing, remote notarization, and transaction-broker representation.
- **Our founding broker's 40-year network** supplies launch sellers, vendors and credibility from day one.

## Business model (structure)
- Sellers: flat, up-front listing tiers (self-serve, pro media, broker-guided).
- Closing services: title and settlement through partners first, then owned.
- Partner services: mortgage, insurance, warranty, moving (RESPA-compliant).
- **Buyers never pay a fee.**

## Go-to-market
1. **10 launch homes** from the founding broker's network, each documented as a case study.
2. **Fiduciary channel:** estate, probate and trust attorneys, CPAs and wealth managers.
3. **Condo associations:** milestone- and SIRS-ready listing packages.
4. **MLS syndication in year one** for portal reach, then Gavel-only exclusive listings as bidder density grows.
5. **Content engine:** live-streamed open houses and auction endings for social video.

## Competition
| | Model | Gap we fill |
|---|---|---|
| Zillow | Sells leads to agents | Can't remove agents without hurting its own revenue |
| Beycome | Flat-fee MLS listing | Sale still happens via private offers |
| Redfin & discount brokers | Cheaper agents | Still a percentage fee |
| Auction.com, Xome | Foreclosure/REO auctions | Distressed inventory, not a consumer experience |
| Opendoor | Buys at a discount | We get sellers market price through competition |

**Moats:** verified-bidder density in each market, exclusive listings, broker reputation and relationships, owned closing data, and a bidding dataset that improves our valuations.
