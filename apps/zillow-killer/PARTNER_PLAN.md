# Gavel Homes: The Founding Broker Plan

*Written for you, Mom. Forty years selling homes in Palm Beach County makes you the person this company can't be built without.*

---

## The short version
I want to build a home marketplace where owners sell directly and buyers bid in the open, like eBay, with a Florida brokerage making sure every sale is done right. **I'd like you to be the founding broker: the license, the judgment, and the reputation behind it.**

This isn't a fight with agents. It's a new way to sell that agents can't easily copy, and you'd be the one who built it.

## Why I think you'll like it
- **You've watched clients lose money and sleep to blind offers.** Open bidding shows the seller exactly what the market will pay, and shows every buyer where they stand.
- **Your expertise becomes the premium option.** Sellers and buyers who want a professional still get one, and it's you and the agents you choose. Today a big part of the job is opening doors and chasing paperwork. Here the platform does that and your team does the advising.
- **Estates, trusts and condos are your world, and they fit this best.** Fiduciaries want a documented, defensible price. Condo buyers after Surfside want the milestone report and reserve study before they commit. We put all of it up front.
- **Northern and seasonal buyers can finally bid with confidence**, through 3D tours, live-streamed walkthroughs and the full document set, without flying down for every showing.
- **It's a legacy.** A Palm Beach broker with four decades of trust launching the next way homes are sold makes a strong story for the press, for clients and for investors.

## How we protect your license
Your license and reputation come first. Every design choice below exists for that reason:
1. **A Florida real estate attorney reviews everything before launch:** contract, auction terms, bidder and seller agreements, and the question of whether auctioneer licensing applies.
2. **Deposits never sit in your trust account.** Bid deposits are card holds, and earnest money goes straight to a title company you choose.
3. **Nothing goes live without your approval.** Every listing, contract and flagged bid goes through your broker console.
4. **Fees are flat, paid up front, and disclosed in writing**, with no hidden compensation and no referral payments (RESPA-clean).
5. **Fair housing is built in:** questions and answers are public to every bidder, there's no demographic data, and ads are targeted carefully.
6. **Wire-fraud protection:** wire instructions only inside the app, confirmed by phone.
7. **Insurance:** E&O, cyber and crime coverage in place before the first listing.

## Questions you'll probably ask
**"You're asking a Realtor to help replace Realtors."**
I'm asking you to help change what an agent is for. Buyers can still bring their own agent, and sellers can still offer a concession. The difference is that nobody is *forced* to pay for door-opening. The judgment and experience you've built over 40 years becomes the thing people choose to pay for.

**"Will other agents in Palm Beach be upset?"**
Some, at first. That's why we use the MLS in year one and follow its rules, and why agents are welcome to bring buyers. A respected broker leading it makes it hard to dismiss.

**"Does anyone actually want to buy a house at auction?"**
The prototype answers the hard parts: verified buying power, an inspection before bidding, a financing and appraisal window, condo/HOA approval built into the contract, and a backup buyer if the winner backs out. We prove it with 10 launch homes before going big.

**"How much of my time will this take?"**
A few hours a week during setup (attorney review, launch homes, introductions). After launch, mostly broker-console reviews, which are designed to take minutes. You decide how much guided-sale work you want.

**"What's in it for me?"**
A founding role and ownership in the company, plus the guided-sale business. We'll set the specific terms together, with an attorney, before anything launches.
