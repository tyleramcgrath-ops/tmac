Hi neighbors, I'm David Tyler. I've spent years in appliance repair, commercial equipment service and facilities maintenance, and I recently moved back to the Palm Beach Gardens and Jupiter area to build Groundswell.

I've worked on everything from refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers and ovens to Sub-Zero, Viking and Miele, plus commercial machines from Taylor, Stoelting, Manitowoc, BUNN and Hamilton Beach.

Working for large companies gave me excellent technical training. It also showed me how I wanted to run my own business: completely straightforward. If something can be repaired reasonably and give you years of use, I'll tell you. If it can't, I'll tell you that too.

Questions

Straight answers

Will you really tell me if it's not worth repairing?

Yes. That's the whole idea behind Groundswell. If replacing makes more sense than repairing, I'll say so, even when it means I don't get the repair job.

Who actually shows up?

Me. Groundswell is owner-operated, so the person you talk to on the phone is the person who diagnoses and fixes your appliance.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Call or text (352) 514-8010 with what's going on and I'll give you a quote.

Do you work on high-end brands like Sub-Zero, Viking and Miele?

Yes. I have experience with premium brands including Sub-Zero, Viking and Miele, along with all the everyday names in most kitchens and laundry rooms.

Do you service commercial equipment?

Yes. I've serviced commercial equipment including Taylor, Stoelting, Manitowoc, BUNN and Hamilton Beach systems. Call to talk through your machine.

What areas do you cover?

Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter and the surrounding areas of northern Palm Beach County.

Can you help with things around the house besides appliances?

Yes. Groundswell also handles handyman work like mounting items on walls, small projects and fences. Ask when you call.
