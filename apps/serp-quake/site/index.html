Why now

Google is feeding rank trackers fake results.

In October 2026 Ahrefs reported that Google has started "poisoning" the results it serves to suspected SERP scrapers, and that 5–20% of results are affected depending on the data provider. Every volatility tool is built on scraped results, and a poisoned result looks exactly like an earthquake: the whole top 10 changes at once.

SERP Quake checks every reading before it counts. If a keyword's results are completely replaced, it searches again, through a second provider when one is available. If the second look matches what was there before, the first was fake and is thrown out. The network reading uses the middle 50% of keywords, so a quarter of them would have to be corrupted in the same direction to move the needle.

How it works

A real seismograph, pointed at Google.

  1. 01

    48 sensors on 8 fault lines

    Keywords in health, finance, ecommerce, local, travel, tech, food and how-to, read every six hours. A niche update lights one fault line. A core update lights them all.

  2. 02

    Ground motion, four ways

    How far the order moved, how many clicks changed hands, which new sites broke in, and whether the page layout changed (AI Overview, snippets, local pack).

  3. 03

    Against each keyword's normal

    Some results churn daily and some never move. Each keyword is measured against its own last 30 quiet days, so only unusual shaking counts.

  4. 04

    A logarithmic magnitude

    M = 2 + 3·log₂(R). A normal day is M2, and every +3 means twice the shaking. A quake is declared the way seismologists do it, with an STA/LTA trigger, and is then checked against Google's own confirmed updates.

Questions

How is this different from Semrush Sensor or MozCast?

Those sensors use private methods and are built on the same scraped results Google is now altering. SERP Quake shows its arithmetic for every reading, throws out results that look poisoned before measuring, and checks its own quakes against Google's confirmed-update record.

What does "M7" actually mean?

Across the whole keyword network, results moved about 3.2 times harder than on a normal day. The scale is logarithmic, so every +3 is a doubling: M5 is 2×, M8 is 4×.

Which Google does it watch?

United States, English, desktop, unpersonalized. Local keywords are pinned to one city, because without that Google picks a different city on every request and local results swing wildly.

Does a big quake mean my site was hit?

It means rankings moved a lot, not who won or lost. Check your own analytics around the quake. The dashboard shows which industries and keywords moved most.

How often does it update?

Every six hours. Core updates roll out over about two weeks, so that is plenty to catch the first tremor, the peak and the aftershocks.
